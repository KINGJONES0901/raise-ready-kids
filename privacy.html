Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated October 3, 2026

Short version: we collect your email so we can send your download and a few helpful emails. We never sell it. We don't collect anything about your kids.

What we collect

  • Your email, when you buy or ask for the free sample pages.
  • Order details (name, email, what you bought). Your card details go straight to our payment processor. We never see or store them.
  • Basic site analytics, like which pages get visited, so we can improve the site. This data doesn't identify you.

What we don't collect

Nothing about your children. The workbooks are printed and filled out at home. None of that ever comes back to us.

How we use it

  • To send your download link and receipt.
  • To send tips and new printables, about one or two emails a week. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To answer you when you email us.

Who we share it with

Only the services we need to run the shop: our payment processor, our email provider and our website host. They're only allowed to use your information to provide their service to us. We never sell or rent your information to anyone.

Cookies

We use a small number of cookies to make checkout work and to understand site traffic. You can block cookies in your browser. Checkout may not work without them.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from emails any time with the link at the bottom of every email.
  • Email hello@raisereadykids.com to see, fix or delete the information we have about you. We'll do it within 30 days.

Children's privacy

This site is for parents and caregivers. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you think a child sent us their information, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. Big changes, we'll email you about.
