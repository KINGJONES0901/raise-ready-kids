# Kids Life Skills Bundle — Curriculum Outline

Working title: **Raise Ready Kids: Kids Life Skills Bundle**
Target: ages 5–12 (two tiers: 5–8 "do it with a grown-up", 9–12 "do it mostly alone")
Target length: 150+ pages across 10 workbooks
Format: US Letter PDF, print-friendly (light backgrounds, no full-bleed color), plus a low-ink version

Every page follows the same three-part rhythm, which the landing page promises:
1. **Explain** — one idea in plain language a kid can read alone (2–4 sentences)
2. **Do** — a 10–15 minute activity: write, draw, cut out, sort, act out
3. **Grown-up tip** — one sentence for the parent: what to say, what not to say

---

## Track A — Feelings & Calm (emotional skills)

### 1. Big Feelings Workbook (~24 pages)
- Feelings wheel: 8 core feelings → 24 shades
- "How big is it?" 1–5 feelings thermometer
- Where I feel it in my body (outline to color)
- Feeling detective: what happened right before?
- Name it → size it → pick a tool (the core loop)
- Feelings vs. actions: "It's okay to feel ___, it's not okay to ___"
- Mad / sad / scared / jealous / embarrassed mini-chapters, 2 pages each
- My feelings plan (one-page summary the kid fills in and posts on the fridge)

### 2. Calm-Down Toolkit (~16 pages)
- "What my brain is doing" — upstairs/downstairs brain explained with a house drawing
- 6 breathing cards (box, balloon, five-finger, dragon, smell-the-flower/blow-the-candle, counting)
- Body scan script for parents to read aloud
- 5-4-3-2-1 senses grounding page
- Calm-down corner setup checklist
- Pick-your-tools menu (kid circles 3 favorites)
- "After the storm" repair page: what I need, what I'll say

### 3. Gratitude & Mindfulness Journal (~32 pages)
- 30 daily two-minute prompts (Being Thankful is Day 1 — the sample page on the site)
- Weekly "noticing walk" page
- Gratitude letter template

### 4. 120 Positive Affirmation Cards (~12 pages, 10 cards per page)
- Four sets of 30: I am brave / I can learn / I am kind / I belong
- Blank cards for the kid to write their own
- Ideas sheet: lunchbox, mirror, bedtime draw

## Track B — Money & Responsibility (independence skills)

### 5. My First Money Workbook (~22 pages)
- Where money comes from (work → pay)
- Coins & bills identification (younger tier)
- Four jars: Spend / Save / Give / Invest (older tier adds Invest)
- Needs vs. wants sort
- Pretend-store game with price tags and play money
- Savings goal tracker (color-in thermometer)
- "Learn to earn" job ideas by age, with a simple pay sheet
- Making change practice (older tier)

### 6. Chore Chart Kit (~14 pages)
- Age-appropriate chore list (5–6, 7–8, 9–10, 11–12)
- Six chart styles: daily checkboxes, weekly grid, picture chart (pre-readers), point system, family chart, "I did it myself" sticker page
- Parent guide: how to hand off a chore (show → do together → watch → let go)
- Allowance or no allowance: one page on both approaches

### 7. Weekly Planner & Routine Builder (~16 pages)
- Morning routine builder (cut-and-stick cards)
- After-school routine
- Bedtime routine
- Weekly planner (reusable; laminate)
- "What's one thing I'm looking forward to?" weekly page
- Time-telling practice (younger tier)

## Track C — Focus & Friends (school & social skills)

### 8. Focus & Follow-Through Pages (~14 pages)
- Big task → small chunks worksheet
- Homework start ritual
- 15-minute timer sheets with "finish line" rewards
- Distraction tally (what pulled me away?)
- "Stuck? Try this" flowchart
- Project planner for school projects

### 9. Friendship & Speaking-Up Workbook (~20 pages)
- How to join a game (three scripts)
- Saying no and meaning it
- Fixing it after a fight: the 4-step apology (what I did, how it affected you, what I'll do differently, what can I do now)
- Being a good sport
- Kind / unkind / bullying: what's the difference and what do I do
- "Tell a grown-up" vs. "handle it myself" sort
- Role-play cards for parent and kid

### 10. Travel & Adventure Journal (~16 pages)
- Road-trip bingo and scavenger hunt
- "Where we're going" map page
- Daily "what I noticed" page
- Waiting-room games (dots and boxes, hangman, would-you-rather)
- Trip memory page

---

## Build order (suggested)
1. Big Feelings Workbook — the flagship, lead with it in marketing
2. Chore Chart Kit — fastest to make, highest everyday use
3. Money Workbook
4. Affirmation Cards
5. Everything else

## Tools to produce it
- Canva (fastest; templates for worksheets and cards) or Affinity Publisher
- Export as PDF/X at 300 DPI; also export a "low-ink" variant with white backgrounds
- Deliver via Gumroad, Payhip or Shopify Digital Downloads; or a free lead magnet via ConvertKit/Kit
